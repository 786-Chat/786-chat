# Food & Safety MS - Restaurant Management System
## Video Tutorial Script (10-12 Minutes)

---

## INTRODUCTION (1 minute)

**[SCREEN: Show the main landing page]**

"Welcome to Food & Safety MS - the complete restaurant management solution designed for restaurant businesses.

This system gives your restaurant its own branded menu page, online ordering, table booking, and a powerful dashboard to manage everything.

The best part? All payments go directly to YOUR Stripe account. There are no commissions, no hidden fees - just a simple monthly subscription.

Let me show you how it all works."

---

## SECTION 1: CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE (3 minutes)

**[SCREEN: Navigate to a restaurant menu page, e.g., /menu/shiraz-afghan]**

### 1.1 The Menu Page

"When your customers visit your restaurant's menu page, they see a beautiful, branded experience.

At the top, you have your restaurant logo, name, and a stunning hero section - you can add images, GIFs, or even videos to showcase your food and restaurant.

Below that, customers can see your menu organized by categories. Each item shows the name, description, price, and a mouth-watering image."

**[SCREEN: Click on a menu item]**

### 1.2 Ordering Process

"When a customer clicks on an item, they can:
- Choose the quantity
- Select any size options like Regular, Large, or Extra Large
- Add extra toppings or customize their order
- Add special instructions

The price updates automatically based on their selections."

**[SCREEN: Add item to basket]**

### 1.3 The Basket

"Items go into the basket. Customers can:
- View all their items
- Change quantities
- Remove items
- See the total price

When ready, they click 'Checkout'."

**[SCREEN: Show checkout process]**

### 1.4 Checkout

"At checkout, customers choose their order type:
- Delivery - food delivered to their address
- Takeaway - they collect from the restaurant
- Dine-in - eating at the restaurant with table number

They enter their contact details - name, phone number, and for delivery, their address.

The system checks if the restaurant is currently open based on your operating hours. If closed, it shows when you'll reopen."

**[SCREEN: Show Stripe payment]**

### 1.5 Payment

"Payment is handled securely through Stripe. The customer enters their card details, and the payment goes directly to YOUR Stripe account - not ours, not a middleman - yours.

Once paid, the order is confirmed and the customer sees their order number."

---

## SECTION 2: RESTAURANT DASHBOARD (4 minutes)

**[SCREEN: Navigate to /admin/dashboard]**

### 2.1 Dashboard Login

"Restaurant owners access their dashboard by logging in with their username and password."

**[SCREEN: Show main dashboard]**

### 2.2 Dashboard Overview

"The dashboard shows you everything at a glance:
- Today's orders count
- Today's revenue
- Recent orders
- Restaurant status (open or closed)

You can also see live statistics and track your business performance."

**[SCREEN: Show orders section]**

### 2.3 Managing Orders

"This is where the magic happens. When a new order comes in:
- You hear an audio notification
- The order appears in the 'New Orders' section
- You see all order details - items, customer info, special requests

You can update the order status:
- Preparing - kitchen is making the food
- Ready - food is ready for collection or delivery
- Completed - order finished

For delivery orders, you can assign a driver and track the delivery in real-time."

**[SCREEN: Show menu management]**

### 2.4 Menu Management

"Managing your menu is simple:
- Add new items with photos, descriptions, and prices
- Create categories to organize your menu
- Set items as available or unavailable
- Add size options and prices
- Create extra toppings that customers can add

You can upload images and GIFs up to 20MB, and videos for special items."

**[SCREEN: Show bookings section]**

### 2.5 Table Bookings

"Customers can book tables through your menu page. In the dashboard you can:
- See all bookings
- Confirm or reject bookings
- View customer details and party size
- Check booking date and time"

---

## SECTION 3: REAL-TIME FEATURES (2 minutes)

**[SCREEN: Show real-time order notification]**

### 3.1 Live Order Notifications

"When a customer places an order:
- You hear an instant audio alert
- The order appears immediately on your dashboard
- No need to refresh - it's all real-time

This means faster service and happier customers."

**[SCREEN: Show driver app]**

### 3.2 Driver Management

"For delivery orders, you can:
- Add drivers with their own login
- Assign orders to drivers
- Track deliveries in real-time
- Drivers get push notifications on their phones

Drivers see their assigned orders, can mark when they've picked up, and when delivered."

**[SCREEN: Show kitchen display]**

### 3.3 Kitchen Display System

"For busy restaurants, the Kitchen Display System (KDS) shows:
- Items organized by station (Pizza, Grill, etc.)
- Chefs mark items complete as they cook
- When all items are done, order automatically moves to 'Ready'

Multiple tablets can be used for different kitchen stations."

## VIDEO RECORDING TIPS

1. **Screen Recording**: Use a tool like Loom, OBS, or Camtasia to record your screen
2. **Microphone**: Use a good quality microphone for clear audio
3. **Script Practice**: Read through the script a few times before recording
4. **Pause Between Sections**: Give yourself time to navigate between screens
5. **AI Voice Option**: Services like ElevenLabs, Murf, or Synthesia can convert this script to AI voice
6. **Background Music**: Add soft background music during editing
