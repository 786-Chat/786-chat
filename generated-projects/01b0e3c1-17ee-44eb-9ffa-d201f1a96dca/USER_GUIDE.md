# FOR CUSTOMERS

## How to Order Food

### Step 1: Visit the Menu
- Go to your restaurant's menu page
- Browse the menu by scrolling down
- Items are organized by categories (Starters, Main Course, Drinks, etc.)

### Step 2: Add Items to Basket
- Click on any item you want to order
- Choose your size (Regular, Large, Extra Large)
- Add any extra toppings you want
- Click "Add to Basket"

### Step 3: Review Your Order
- Click on the basket icon
- Check all your items are correct
- Change quantities if needed
- Remove items you don't want

### Step 4: Checkout
- Click "Checkout"
- Choose order type:
  - **Delivery** - We bring food to you
  - **Takeaway** - You collect from restaurant
  - **Dine-in** - Eat at the restaurant
- Enter your name and phone number
- For delivery: Enter your full address

### Step 5: Pay
- Enter your card details
- Click "Pay Now"
- You'll see your order number
- Wait for your food!

---

## How to Book a Table

### Step 1: Go to Booking Page
- Click "Book Table" on the menu page

### Step 2: Choose Date & Time
- Select the date you want to visit
- Choose your preferred time

### Step 3: Enter Details
- Number of guests
- Your name
- Phone number
- Any special requests

### Step 4: Confirm
- Click "Book Table"
- You'll receive confirmation

# FOR RESTAURANT OWNERS

## Logging In

1. Go to your dashboard login page
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click "Login"

---

## Managing Orders

### View New Orders
- New orders appear at the top
- You hear a sound when new order arrives
- Click on order to see details

### Update Order Status
- **Preparing** - Click when kitchen starts cooking
- **Ready** - Click when food is ready
- **Completed** - Click when customer receives food

### For Delivery Orders
- Assign a driver from the dropdown
- Track delivery progress on map

---

## Managing Your Menu

### Add New Item
1. Go to "Menu" section
2. Click "Add Item"
3. Fill in:
   - Item name
   - Description
   - Price
   - Category
4. Upload a photo (up to 20MB)
5. Click "Save"

### Edit Item
1. Find the item in menu list
2. Click "Edit"
3. Make your changes
4. Click "Save"

### Hide/Show Item
- Toggle "Available" switch to hide item from customers
- Useful when item is sold out

### Add Size Options
1. Click on menu item
2. Go to "Variants" tab
3. Add sizes like:
   - Regular - £5.99
   - Large - £7.99
   - Extra Large - £9.99

### Add Extra Toppings
1. Click on menu item
2. Go to "Modifiers" tab
3. Add toppings like:
   - Extra Cheese - £1.00
   - Jalapeños - £0.50

---

## Managing Table Bookings

### View Bookings
- Go to "Bookings" section
- See all reservations with date, time, guests

### Confirm/Reject Booking
- Click on a booking
- Click "Confirm" or "Reject"
- Customer will be notified

## Managing Drivers

### Add a Driver
1. Go to "Drivers" section
2. Click "Add Driver"
3. Enter:
   - Driver name
   - Phone number
   - Username
   - Password
4. Click "Save"

### Assign Order to Driver
1. Click on a delivery order
2. Select driver from dropdown
3. Driver receives notification on their phone

---

## Updating Restaurant Details

### Change Logo
1. Go to "Settings" or "Branding"
2. Click on logo area
3. Upload new logo (up to 20MB)
4. Click "Save"

### Update Operating Hours
1. Go to "Settings" or "Hours"
2. Set opening and closing times for each day
3. Set different hours for delivery and collection
4. Click "Save"

### Add Hero Images/Videos
1. Go to "Hero Gallery" section
2. Click "Add Image" or "Add Video"
3. Upload file (images up to 20MB, videos up to 100MB)
4. Drag to reorder
5. Click "Save"

# TROUBLESHOOTING

## Common Issues

### "Payment Failed"
- Check card details are correct
- Make sure card has sufficient funds
- Try a different card

### "Restaurant Closed"
- Check the operating hours
- Order will show when restaurant reopens

### "Can't Login"
- Check username and password
- Contact your provider if forgotten

### "Image Not Uploading"
- Check file is under 20MB
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP

### "No Sound for New Orders"
- Check device volume is on
- Allow sound notifications in browser
